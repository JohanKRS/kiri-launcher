Kiri Launcher privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026.

Kiri Launcher (io.github.johankrs.kiri) is an open-source launcher for Android. It does not collect, store off the device or share personal data. It has no account, sign-in, ads or usage analytics, and uses no third-party service for any of that.

What stays on the device

Everything Kiri keeps stays on the device, in the app's own data: the home screen, the settings, the icons you chose, the list of stocks and currencies in the Market widget and the keys you paste for the quote services. Android's automatic backup (to your Google account) may include this data, as it does for any app; Kiri has no access to that backup. Kiri's own backup is a file you save wherever you choose, and the keys only go into it, locked with a password, if you turn that option on.

Permissions and what they are for

  • See installed apps: to show your apps in the drawer and on the home screen, the widgets they offer and icon packs. The list never leaves the device.
  • Internet: used only by the Market widget, to fetch prices from the services you chose (brapi.dev, Alpaca, Finnhub, AwesomeAPI, Frankfurter). These requests carry only the code of the stock or currency and, when the service asks for one, your own key. Each service has its own privacy policy.
  • All files access (optional): Android only hands the wallpaper picture to apps with this permission. Kiri uses it only to read the wallpaper and blur its copy behind the glass, and to find Kiri's own backups saved on the device. It reads and sends no other file.
  • Photos (TV only, optional): to choose a picture on the device as the home screen background.
  • Wallpaper: to apply the picture you choose in Kiri.
  • Accessibility service (optional): so a gesture can lock the screen and, on a TV, so the Home button can bring up Kiri. It cannot read what is on the screen. It only appears in Android when you choose one of these, and before opening Android's Accessibility settings Kiri explains it on a screen of its own and only goes on if you allow it. On a phone it receives no events. On a TV, while you have Kiri on the Home button, it is told only when a window of the TV's own home screen comes up, and of no other app, so Kiri can come up in its place.
  • Open the notification shade and ask to uninstall an app: actions you tap.

The Google Play version does not ask for all files access, the full list of apps or photos. There, the glass blurs only a wallpaper chosen in Kiri itself.

Other apps

A separately installed app can offer quotes to the Market widget. Kiri only asks it for the prices on your list, and only after you choose that app as a source.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/JohanKRS/kiri-launcher/issues.
