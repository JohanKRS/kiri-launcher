On your phone

Your screen, your way.

  • A grid that fits you

    Rows, columns and sizes are all adjustable, always with a live preview. App names wrap onto two lines instead of being cut.

  • Folders and pages

    Folders open into a square glass panel. Add, remove and reorder pages, and hide the apps you never open.

  • A Market widget

    Stocks and currencies with charts, a panel that opens from the widget itself and a currency converter. Prices come straight from the services you pick.

  • Gestures you choose

    Swipe for the drawer or the notifications, and decide what each gesture does, like a double tap to lock the screen.

  • Icons, your way

    Circle, rounded corners or squircle, any size, glass behind them, and icon packs. New apps go to the drawer and never clutter your screen.

  • Your own backup

    Your whole setup in one file, saved wherever you want. Move to a new phone and bring your home screen with you.

On your TV

A home screen made for the couch.

The same Kiri becomes a TV home when it opens on Android TV, with everything done by remote.

  • Banners in a grid or in a carousel
  • HDMI inputs with ready-made banners
  • Clock and Market widgets
  • Its own wallpapers, or a picture of yours

Nothing about you leaves your phone.

Kiri has no account, no ads and no usage analytics, and it collects no data. Your layout and settings stay on the device. The only thing that goes online is the Market widget, asking the quote services you chose for the prices on your list.

Free, and open to everyone.

Kiri is free software under the GPL-3.0 license. No ads, no in-app purchases, nothing locked. The code is on GitHub for anyone to read, use or improve.
